3BHK Interior Design Cost in Mumbai

Planning interiors for a 3BHK in Mumbai? Understand the major cost components, room-wise budget ranges and the difference between essential interiors and complete turnkey execution before starting your project.

What should you budget for a 3BHK interior?

A 3BHK usually offers more rooms, storage opportunities and design possibilities than a smaller apartment, which also means the project scope can vary considerably. Kitchen size, wardrobes, beds, study units, TV furniture, ceiling work, lighting and decorative finishes all influence the final quotation.


Instead of relying only on a single per-square-foot number, it is more useful to identify the actual furniture, finishes and execution work required in each room. This gives you a clearer understanding of where the budget is being allocated.

Cost Factors

What affects the cost of a 3BHK interior?

Two 3BHK apartments with similar carpet areas can still have very different quotations. The number of custom elements and the specifications selected usually make the biggest difference.

1. Carpet Area

Larger apartments generally require more furniture, ceiling work, lighting, materials and finishing.

2. Storage Requirements

Additional wardrobes, lofts, crockery units, shoe storage and utility cabinetry can significantly increase the scope.

3. Kitchen Design

Kitchen size, hardware, accessories, shutter finish and countertop selections can substantially affect the total budget.

4. Material Selection

Plywood, laminates, veneers, acrylic surfaces, hardware brands and decorative materials all influence cost.

5. Civil & Electrical Work

Wall changes, flooring, plumbing, waterproofing and electrical modifications add to the overall execution requirement.

6. Custom Design

Feature walls, bespoke furniture, panelling, complex ceilings and premium details require additional fabrication and installation.

What can a complete 3BHK interior include?

  • Wardrobes and loft storage for bedrooms

  • Dining or crockery storage
  • Study or work-from-home furniture
  • False ceiling and lighting coordination
  • Electrical changes
  • Painting and wall finishes
  • Civil and renovation work where required
  • Material procurement
  • Execution management
  • Final finishing and handover

Budget Planning

Can you complete a 3BHK interior within ₹8 lakh?

A controlled 3BHK interior may be planned around this budget when essential requirements are prioritised and material specifications are carefully selected. The budget can first focus on the kitchen, bedroom wardrobes, necessary storage and basic living-room furniture.

Where to spend first

  • Kitchen hardware and frequently used fittings
  • Bedroom wardrobes and functional storage
  • Electrical planning before furniture installation
  • Durable materials in high-use areas
  • Storage that genuinely improves everyday use

Where the budget can be controlled

  • Keep ceiling designs straightforward
  • Limit decorative panelling
  • Use practical laminate finishes where suitable
  • Prioritise fixed furniture over décor
  • Add non-essential decorative upgrades in a later phase
