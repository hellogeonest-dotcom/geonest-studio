How Much Do Civil Contractors in Mumbai Charge?

Civil contracting costs in Mumbai depend on the type of work, site conditions, quantities, materials, labour requirements and execution scope. A proper quotation is prepared after understanding the site and required work.

What Work Does a Civil Contractor Handle?

Civil contractors can handle work such as masonry, plastering, waterproofing, flooring, tiling, demolition, repairs, painting and other agreed civil and finishing activities depending on the project scope.

Do Civil Contractors in Mumbai Also Handle Renovation?

Yes. Civil contracting can form part of a residential or commercial renovation, including demolition, alterations, masonry, waterproofing, flooring, tiling and finishing work.
