Renovation FAQs

Common questions about home renovation in Mumbai.

How Much Does Home Renovation Cost in Mumbai?

Home renovation costs in Mumbai depend on the size of the property, existing conditions, demolition requirements, civil work, flooring, waterproofing, finishes, interior scope, materials and level of customisation. A project-specific quotation is prepared after site assessment.

What Is Included in Home Renovation?

Depending on the agreed scope, home renovation can include demolition, alterations, masonry, plastering, waterproofing, flooring, tiling, repairs, painting, finishing and interior coordination.

Can You Renovate an Old Apartment in Mumbai?

Yes. Old apartments can be renovated based on their existing condition, building restrictions and approved scope. The work may include demolition, repairs, civil alterations, waterproofing, flooring, finishes and interior upgrades.
