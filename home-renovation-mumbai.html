Plan your budget

A renovation estimate should be based on scope, not guesswork.

Costs depend on the home’s size and condition, material choices, extent of civil changes, custom furniture, services and site logistics. A measured, item-wise estimate is more useful than a generic per-square-foot promise.

  • Property size, room count and existing condition
  • Required demolition and civil modifications
  • Furniture quantity, dimensions and finishes
  • Electrical, plumbing and ceiling requirements
  • Material selection, access and project timeline
Before commencement

Keep the scope and changes documented.

The proposal should state what is included and excluded, specifications, payment milestones and responsibilities. Hidden conditions and client-requested changes can affect both cost and schedule.

Structural alterations require review by a qualified structural professional and applicable permissions. Additional work should be separately estimated and approved in writing before it begins.
FAQs

Home renovation in Mumbai — common questions.

How much does home renovation cost in Mumbai?

There is no single reliable rate for every home. Cost depends on the scope, existing condition, materials, furniture, services and site constraints. An item-wise estimate after understanding the property is the better approach.

Can I renovate only my kitchen or one bedroom?

Yes, individual-room upgrades may be considered subject to location, scope and scheduling. Share the details so the project can be assessed.

Can I live in the home during renovation?

That depends on the work area, dust and noise, water and power interruptions, safety and project sequencing. We can discuss practical phasing, but some work may require the area to be vacated.

Do you handle design and execution together?

GeoNest Studio can coordinate the defined design and execution scope, with inclusions and responsibilities documented in the proposal.

Which areas do you serve?

We consider suitable projects across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai and Thane, depending on scope and availability.
