Start with the scope

Interior cost is more than a per-square-foot number.

The final budget depends on the carpet area, existing site condition, number of rooms, storage requirements, material specifications, hardware, lighting, civil changes and the level of design and execution support required.

Two homes of similar size can have different budgets because their layouts, family needs and finish expectations are different. A useful estimate starts with a clearly defined scope—not a headline rate alone.

What influences your budget?

  • Carpet area and layout complexity
  • Kitchen, wardrobe and storage quantity
  • Plywood, laminate and surface specifications
  • Hardware, fittings and accessories
  • Electrical, plumbing and civil modifications
  • False ceiling, painting and flooring
  • Furniture, furnishings and appliances
  • Design, drawings and project coordination
Plan room by room

Build your estimate around actual requirements.

Living & dining

TV unit, storage, feature wall, ceiling, lighting, dining furniture and flooring.

Bedrooms

Wardrobes, beds, side tables, dressers, study units, lofts and soft furnishings.

Kitchen

Base and wall cabinets, tall units, countertop, backsplash, hardware and accessories.

Bathrooms

Plumbing changes, waterproofing, tiles, sanitaryware, fittings and mirrors where included.

Civil & electrical

Demolition, masonry, flooring, wiring, switches, lighting points and finishing work.

Design & execution

Space planning, 3D visualisation, working drawings, procurement and site coordination.

Compare quotations carefully

Check what is included before comparing totals.

CheckWhat to confirm
CarpentryPlywood grade and thickness, laminate brand, internal finish, edge banding and hardware model.
KitchenCabinet layout, accessories, tandem units, countertop and sink/tap inclusions.
Civil workExact areas, material specifications, debris removal and surface preparation.
ElectricalPoint count, wire and switch brands, fixtures and any concealed work.

Ask every contractor to identify inclusions, exclusions, assumptions and any provisional amounts in writing.

Frequently asked questions

Interior Design Cost in Mumbai — FAQs

How much does interior design cost in Mumbai?

There is no single rate that suits every home. Cost depends on area, scope, material specifications, furniture quantity, civil and electrical requirements, and the level of design and execution support. A site-specific scope is needed for a reliable estimate.

Is interior design charged separately from execution?

It can be. Some projects require design-only services, while others combine design, procurement and execution in a turnkey scope. Confirm the fee structure and responsibilities in the written proposal.

What should an interior quotation include?

It should clearly state quantities or dimensions, material brands and specifications, hardware, labour, civil and electrical inclusions, design deliverables, exclusions, taxes and payment milestones.
