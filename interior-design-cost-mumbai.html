Interior Design Cost in Mumbai

Trying to understand how much home interiors cost in Mumbai? This guide breaks down the major budget categories, room-wise costs and key factors that influence interior quotations before you start design and execution.

How much does interior design cost?

There is no single fixed price for interior design because the scope can vary dramatically from one home to another. A project focused only on essential kitchen and wardrobe work will cost very differently from a complete turnkey interior with custom furniture, false ceiling, lighting, civil modifications and decorative finishes.


The most useful way to estimate your interior budget is to first understand your apartment size, list the rooms and furniture you require, identify the level of finish you want and then compare quotations based on the same scope.

Cost Factors

What affects interior design cost in Mumbai?

The final cost depends much more on the actual scope and specifications than on apartment size alone.

1. Carpet Area

A larger home typically requires more furniture, materials, ceiling work, lighting and finishing.

2. Furniture Quantity

Wardrobes, lofts, kitchen cabinets, TV units, studies and storage systems directly affect project value.

3. Materials

Plywood, laminates, veneers, acrylic finishes, hardware and surface materials can create large differences in quotations.

4. Kitchen Specifications

Hardware, accessories, cabinet quantity, countertop and finish selections strongly influence kitchen cost.

5. Civil Modifications

Flooring, tiling, plumbing, waterproofing, wall changes and electrical modifications add to the execution scope.

6. Customisation

Bespoke furniture, decorative panelling, feature walls and complex ceiling details require additional labour and material.

Design vs Turnkey

Design-only or complete turnkey interior?

Design-only service

A design-focused engagement may include space planning, furniture layouts, visualisation, material direction and execution drawings. The client can then appoint vendors or contractors separately for execution.

Turnkey interior service

A turnkey project can combine design, procurement, vendor coordination, material sourcing and site execution under one coordinated scope.

Budget Planning

How to control your interior budget.

A successful budget does not necessarily mean choosing the cheapest option everywhere. It means deciding which elements deserve more investment and where simpler specifications will work perfectly well.

Prioritise

  • Frequently used kitchen hardware
  • Functional wardrobes and storage
  • Good electrical planning
  • Durable materials in high-use areas
  • Furniture that directly improves everyday function

Control spending on

  • Overly complex false ceilings
  • Unnecessary decorative wall panelling
  • Premium finishes in low-use areas
  • Excessive custom furniture
  • Decorative elements that can be added later

Frequently Asked Questions

Interior design cost FAQs.

How much does interior design cost in Mumbai?

The cost depends on apartment size, furniture quantities, kitchen scope, wardrobes, materials, lighting, ceiling work, civil modifications and the level of customisation.

What is the biggest factor affecting interior cost?

Furniture quantity and specifications are usually among the biggest factors. Kitchen cabinets, wardrobes, storage units, plywood grades, hardware and surface finishes can significantly change the quotation.

Is turnkey interior more expensive than design-only?

Turnkey projects generally have a larger total value because they may include materials, labour, procurement, coordination and execution in addition to design services.

Should I compare interiors using price per square foot?

Per-square-foot figures can be useful for rough comparisons, but they can be misleading when scopes differ. Comparing actual furniture quantities, materials and included work gives a clearer picture.
