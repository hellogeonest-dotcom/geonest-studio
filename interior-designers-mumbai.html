Plan your investment

Interior design costs depend on the details.

The overall budget is influenced by home size, room count, furniture quantities, material grades, civil modifications, lighting and finishing choices. We recommend defining the scope before comparing estimates.

  • Property size and existing condition
  • Number of rooms and storage requirements
  • Furniture design, dimensions and hardware
  • Material brands, finishes and accessories
  • Civil, electrical, ceiling and painting scope

FAQs

Choosing an interior designer in Mumbai.

How do I choose the right interior designer?

Review the designer’s process, scope clarity, material specifications, communication style and ability to coordinate execution. Ask what is included, excluded and how changes are handled.

Do you provide 3D designs before execution?

Visualisation deliverables are discussed and confirmed in the proposal. The number of views and revision rounds should be agreed before the design stage begins.

Can you design and execute the full home?

GeoNest Studio can coordinate a defined end-to-end residential interior scope, subject to project suitability and the approved proposal.

What should I share to get started?

Share your location, floor plan, property size, room requirements, reference images and indicative budget. This helps us understand the project before the consultation.

Which areas do you cover?

We consider projects across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai and Thane, subject to scope and availability.
