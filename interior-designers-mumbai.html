Interiors designed for Mumbai living.

Designing a space in Mumbai often means making every square foot work harder. Apartment layouts, storage requirements, natural light, existing site conditions and building restrictions all influence the final result.


Our approach begins by understanding how you use the space, what you want to improve and the budget you are comfortable working with. We then develop a clear design direction that balances aesthetics, function and practical execution.
