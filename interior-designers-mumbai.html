How Much Do Interior Designers in Mumbai Charge?

Interior design fees in Mumbai vary depending on the project size, scope, number of rooms, design requirements, drawings, 3D visualisation and execution involvement. A final fee is best determined after understanding the property and required scope.

What Services Does an Interior Designer in Mumbai Provide?

Depending on the agreed scope, an interior designer may provide space planning, furniture layouts, material selection, design development, 3D visualisation, working drawings, lighting and electrical coordination and execution support.

Do Interior Designers in Mumbai Also Handle Execution?

Some interior designers provide design-only services while others also coordinate or manage execution. GeoNest Studio offers interior design services along with optional turnkey execution and project coordination depending on the agreed scope.
