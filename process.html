How the journey works

From understanding your space to planning the details.

Every property and project is different. The sequence below is a general guide for interior design, renovation and turnkey enquiries. The exact deliverables, timelines and responsibilities are confirmed according to the selected services and approved scope.

01Discovery

Initial Consultation

We discuss your property, lifestyle or business needs, preferred design direction, approximate budget, timeline and the type of support you are looking for.

02Assessment

Site Study & Measurements

Where required and agreed, the site is reviewed for dimensions, existing conditions, services, access and practical execution considerations.

03Planning

Design & Space Planning

Layouts, furniture planning, design references, material direction and visual concepts are developed according to the brief and agreed design scope.

04Documentation

Scope, BOQ & Estimate

The proposed work is documented with relevant specifications, quantities, inclusions, exclusions and commercial details for review and approval.

05Preparation

Approvals & Coordination

Before execution, the agreed drawings, finishes, materials, schedule, payment milestones and project responsibilities are confirmed with the client.

06Execution

Site Work & Reviews

Work proceeds as per the approved scope. Site activities, vendors, materials and quality observations are coordinated according to the project arrangement.

07Completion

Finishing & Snag Review

Finishing items are reviewed, identified snags are recorded and agreed rectification or completion actions are coordinated.

08Handover

Final Handover

After completion of the agreed work and applicable checks, the project is prepared for handover with relevant closing details.

Scope clarity matters: Site visits, drawings, 3D visualisations, BOQ preparation, procurement, execution supervision and handover support depend on the service package and written agreement. Any additional work or changes are discussed and approved before proceeding.
