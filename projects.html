From concept to reality

Every project begins with the actual site.

01

Requirement Study

Understanding the people, routines, priorities, site conditions, existing structure and overall project goals.

02

Spatial Direction

Developing layouts, circulation, furniture planning and an overall design mood based on how the space needs to function.

03

Material & Detail Development

Selecting practical finishes, lighting, joinery and execution details while keeping the agreed project budget in consideration.

04

Execution Planning

Translating the approved design direction into drawings, scope, procurement requirements and coordinated site execution.
