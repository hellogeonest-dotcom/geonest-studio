Before work begins

What we clarify in the scope.

  • Item-wise description and measurable quantities
  • Material grade, make and finish where specified
  • Labour, transport and debris-removal responsibility
  • Site protection and access arrangements
  • Work sequence, dependencies and indicative timeline
  • Testing, approvals and handover requirements
Safety & responsibility

The right work, with the right checks.

Structural alterations, load-bearing changes, major plumbing or electrical interventions and regulated activities require assessment by appropriately qualified professionals and relevant approvals. They should never be undertaken on assumption alone.

The final proposal will identify inclusions and exclusions. Hidden site conditions or client-requested changes may affect the estimate and schedule; any additional work should be approved in writing before it starts.
FAQs

Common questions about civil work.

Do you undertake complete home renovation?

GeoNest Studio can coordinate defined renovation and interior-related civil work as part of a project. The exact scope is assessed based on location, size and requirements.

Can you provide a civil work estimate from photographs?

Photographs and drawings help with an initial discussion, but a reliable item-wise estimate may require a site visit, measurements and confirmation of material specifications.

Do you handle structural changes?

Structural changes are not treated as routine civil work. They require review and design by a qualified structural professional, along with applicable permissions, before execution.

Do you serve Navi Mumbai and Thane?

GeoNest Studio serves projects across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai and Thane, subject to project suitability and scope.

How can I get started?

Share your location, property type, photographs, floor plan and a brief description of the work. We can discuss the next steps for assessment and estimation.
