Design with purpose

Residential & commercial interior design.

Every space has its own needs. Our interior design process begins by understanding the property, its users, existing conditions, layout possibilities, preferred style and budget priorities. From there, the agreed design scope can develop into a coordinated direction for the space.

Depending on the project and selected services, this may include space planning, furniture layouts, mood direction, 3D visualisation, material and finish selection, lighting concepts, design drawings and execution coordination.

For apartment projects, explore our 2BHK interior design cost guide, 3BHK interior design cost guide and Mumbai interior design cost guide.

What the design scope can include

Thought through from layout to detail.

The deliverables below are selected according to the property, project requirements and approved proposal. Not every item applies to every engagement.

01 / PLANNING

Space Planning

Layout studies, circulation, furniture placement and practical use of available space.

02 / VISUAL DIRECTION

Concept & 3D Visualisation

Design references, mood direction, colour palette and visual concepts to communicate the proposed look and feel.

03 / MATERIALS

Material & Finish Selection

Guidance on laminates, finishes, surfaces, hardware and other selections relevant to the approved design.

04 / DETAILING

Furniture & Joinery Drawings

Relevant furniture layouts, storage planning and detailing for agreed custom furniture elements.

05 / SERVICES

Lighting & Electrical Layout

Design coordination for lighting points, electrical requirements and functional placement as included in the scope.

06 / COORDINATION

Execution Support

Coordination with site teams, vendors and execution requirements where included in the agreed engagement.

Our design journey

A clear sequence from brief to design development.

Good design is a collaborative process. Key decisions and deliverables are reviewed with the client at agreed stages.

01

Understand

Discuss property, lifestyle, requirements, design preferences and budget.

02

Plan

Review site conditions, measurements, layouts and functional requirements.

03

Develop

Build the design direction, visualisations, material selections and relevant drawings.

04

Coordinate

Confirm the agreed design information and next steps for execution, where included.

Scope and revisions: Drawings, 3D views, revision limits, site visits and execution support are defined in the project proposal. Any additional design changes or work outside the approved scope are discussed and approved separately before proceeding.
