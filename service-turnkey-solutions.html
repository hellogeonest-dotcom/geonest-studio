Clarity before commencement

What you should expect in a proposal.

  • Room-wise scope and item descriptions
  • Material brands, grades, finishes and specifications
  • Drawings and visualisation deliverables
  • Commercials, payment milestones and validity
  • Indicative schedule and project dependencies
  • Inclusions, exclusions and change-order process
Important to know

No surprises through unclear assumptions.

Items not specifically listed in the approved scope should be treated as excluded until quoted and approved in writing. Site conditions, society permissions, structural changes, appliance purchases and statutory charges may require separate consideration.

Any additional work or change in specification should be discussed, priced and approved before execution. Timelines depend on site readiness, approvals, material availability and the agreed project schedule.
FAQs

Turnkey interiors, explained.

What does turnkey interior work mean?

It is a coordinated service covering the agreed design, material, labour, fabrication and execution activities through to handover. Exact inclusions vary by project and are listed in the approved scope.

Can I choose materials and finishes?

Yes. Materials and finishes are discussed during design development and documented for approval before procurement or execution.

Can I add work after the project starts?

Additional work can be considered. It should be separately estimated and approved in writing, including any impact on cost and timeline, before commencement.

Do you take up projects across Mumbai and Navi Mumbai?

GeoNest Studio serves projects across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai and Thane, subject to project size, location and scope suitability.

How do I get a project estimate?

Share your location, property size, room requirements, photographs or floor plan, and budget range. We can then discuss the next steps and the information needed for an estimate.
